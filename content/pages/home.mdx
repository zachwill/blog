# About Me

I'm currently **Director of Data Science** for the [Portland Trail Blazers](https://www.nba.com/blazers/zach-williams). I've been working with data, building products, and solving problems for over a decade.

I write about data science, product development, basketball analytics, and the occasional random thought. Most of my posts are short observations or lessons learned along the way.

You can find me on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/zachwill) where I share thoughts on data science and basketball, or check out my projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/zachwill). Feel free to reach out at [hey@zachwill.com](mailto:hey@zachwill.com).
